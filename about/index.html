The Forgemaster

Science in the Fire

Meet the Forgemaster, Adam: an evidence-based exercise performance and nutrition coach, as well as a researcher dedicated to helping individuals transform their health, body composition, and physical performance.

Grounded in advanced academic training in exercise science, cellular biology, and clinical nutrition, Adam bridges the gap between cutting-edge science and practical, real-world application. As a coach, he holds top-tier certifications from world-renowned certification bodies and multiple university degrees, so you can be sure every training and nutrition strategy is well-tailored, effective, and rooted in high-standard practice.

With nearly seven years of experience across clinical research, university instruction, and wellness coordination, Adam brings a comprehensive understanding of human physiology and clinical nutrition to every client. Whether heading exercise and nutrition consulting for major research studies or working one-on-one with individuals, his passion lies in empowering clients to achieve sustainable weight loss, build strength, optimize overall wellness, and crafting the ideal physique for you.

Adam takes a holistic, science-driven and evidence-based approach to coaching, not only to move people to the goal line, but to equip them with the tools they need to keep running once they pass the finish line.
